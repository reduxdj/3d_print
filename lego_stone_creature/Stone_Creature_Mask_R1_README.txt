DIMENSIONS
Overall assembled height including stud: 300 mm.
Outside diameter: 224.8 mm.
Main shell thickness: 2.4 mm, with thicker joint rails and magnet housings.
Wearer's supplied head circumference: 600 mm.
Minimum clear head passage through the magnetic rim: 200 mm diameter.
Neck opening: 133.69 mm diameter / 420 mm circumference.
Eye holes: 30.15 mm diameter, 67.5 mm center to center.
Nose hole: 14 x 5 mm, centered 172 mm below the top of the stud.
Stud: exact original armor stud geometry; 55.7 mm outside diameter, 38.8 mm inside diameter, 18 mm height.

PRINTED PARTS AND HARDWARE
1 top/head, with integrated stud: 20 magnet pockets.
1 lower left half: 16 magnet pockets.
1 lower right half: 16 magnet pockets.
Left and right labels refer to the view from the front of the mask.
52 disc magnets, 6 mm diameter x 3 mm thick, total.
Glue suitable for the magnets and PLA.
Optional foam lining, selected after checking wearer clearance.
No screws, separate pocket covers or decorative overlays are included.

MAGNET CONNECTIONS
Each lower half has 10 magnets on its horizontal rim, matching 10 in the top.
The front vertical seam has three pairs; the rear seam has three pairs.
Pocket size: 6.25 mm diameter x 3.15 mm deep. Set the magnet faces flush with the mating surfaces when gluing.
Mark polarity before gluing. Each opposing pair must attract.
The locating lip guides assembly; it is not intended as a snap fit.

PRINT SETUP
Use the 3MF as a project at 100% scale.
Plate 1: top inverted, stud down, with support beneath the crown.
Plates 2 and 3: lower halves upright, horizontal rim pockets facing up.
0.20 mm layers; 4 walls; 5 top and bottom layers; 12% gyroid infill.
Automatic tree supports from the build plate; 5 mm outer brim.
All three parts fit within the A1's configured 256 mm build volume.
The slice report gives the time and filament estimate for each plate, including supports and brims.
The 3MF contains the models and settings; slice the desired plate in Bambu Studio before printing.

FIT AND ASSEMBLY
First check the full-size eye and opening guides against the wearer. A head circumference alone does not establish front-to-back head depth. Padding changes the eye position.
After printing, remove supports and brims and dry-fit the three parts before installing magnets.
Glue the magnets in matching polarity, keeping adhesive out of the locating lip. Let the adhesive cure fully before assembly.
The two lower halves close around the neck and join the top; leave their mating seams removable.
The eye and nose openings pass through the front shell. The inner back wall may be visible through them in the renders.

VERIFICATION
Each exported part is one closed, consistently oriented mesh with no non-manifold edges or degenerate triangles.
The assembled pieces do not intersect; the top lifts clear of the locating rim.
All 52 nominal magnets fit the modeled recesses without intersecting plastic.
The stud above the crown matches the original source mesh.
Digital checks do not establish physical fit, magnet retention or strength; this is an untested wearable prototype.
