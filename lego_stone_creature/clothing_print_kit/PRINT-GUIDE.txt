The chest, pendant, bone chain, molten waist detail, cracks, and both sets of
leg markings have been redrawn from the photo. Soft photographic edges were
cleaned into flat colors. Head, hands, toy outline, and the separate translucent
shoulder armor are not included in this fabric-print artwork.

EDITING
All SVG artwork consists of closed filled paths in named editable groups.
No raster images, live text, live strokes, linked assets, or background shapes.
The leg designs are intentionally different, matching the reference.
Open in Illustrator, Inkscape, or another SVG-capable vector editor. Check the
physical dimensions after import. The master contains all three panels; the
individual files make independent placement and production easier.

PLACEMENT
Center the chest graphic on the front of the shirt. Position each leg panel
on the front of the corresponding pant leg, with the small lower crack detail
above the hem. Keep clear of side seams and hems. The shirt/pants drawing in
the preview is illustrative, not a garment cutting or sewing pattern.
Adult medium is the intended fit; check the 33 cm width on the actual shirt.

PRODUCTION
Files are supplied in normal viewing orientation. Apply transfer mirroring
only if your production process requires it. Backgrounds are transparent;
fabric shows through the unprinted spaces. Light ivory and stone areas are
printed colors, not holes. Colors are flat RGB, including the warm highlights.
A print shop should apply its usual color management and white underbase for
the chosen fabric and print process. No registration marks or bleed are added.
